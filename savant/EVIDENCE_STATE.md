# SAVANT Evidence State v0.4

SAVANT can now summarize the **shape of the recorded evidence** for the selected claim:

- total evidence records
- SUPPORTS records
- CONTRADICTS records
- PRIMARY records
- UNKNOWN source-class records
- unresolved research questions

It may also surface literal gaps such as “No primary-source evidence has been recorded.”

These are database-state observations, not epistemic verdicts. In particular:

- zero recorded contradictions does not mean no contradictory evidence exists;
- a high count of supporting records does not establish truth;
- PRIMARY is a provenance class, not a reliability score;
- the summary does not calculate confidence or recommend a conclusion.

The purpose is to make missing research visible without pretending that completeness can be inferred from the local collection.
