# CAPITAL v0.1

CAPITAL is EDGE's lawful liquidity and funding layer.

Its job is not to pretend software can manufacture money. Its job is to help turn real opportunities and resources into **spendable, controlled capital**.

Initial source classes:
- EARNED
- SALE
- GRANT
- RECEIVABLE
- FINANCING
- INVESTMENT
- OTHER

States:
- PIPELINE — possible/expected capital, not spendable cash
- AVAILABLE — user-confirmed liquid capital
- CLOSED — no longer active

The dashboard deliberately separates AVAILABLE from PIPELINE so expected money is never represented as money already in hand.

Future modules can connect RADAR opportunities, receivables, sales pipelines, grants, legitimate financing offers, treasury/cash management, and accounting data. Any automated financial action should require explicit authorization, preserve records, and expose fees, interest, dilution, restrictions and risk.
