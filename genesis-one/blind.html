Randomized hidden regimes, unknown programs, honest abstention, and full-loop timing.

Each trial: 240 tasks with randomly changing regimes (12–36 tasks), 15% tasks from a hidden unknown family. The learner sees only previous outcomes, never the current family or regime.

All target families are defined in benchmark code; “unknown” means absent from candidate library, not a discovery. Correctness means matching six sample points. Failure to solve an unknown task must be reported as abstention. Wall-clock measurements are noisy and exclude task generation; policy and ordering are included for adaptive. Fixed and adaptive strategies use identical task sequences within each trial.
