All strategies share 176 candidate programs and the same acceptance threshold from v15. Out-of-distribution tasks comprise approximately 10%. The VOI policy uses a shortlist of up to 6 plausible candidates and scores 8 candidate locations by bounded pairwise disagreement, divided by one plus a small computation penalty. It stops early only when no location offers enough disagreement to justify another measurement. This is a heuristic VOI proxy, not a calibrated expected utility model. Scores are not probabilities. Ground truth is inaccessible to decisions and used only in evaluation. Abstract cost counts candidate comparisons, measurement requests, and selection predictions; sorting is excluded.
