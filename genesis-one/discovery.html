Genesis ONE v10 · Discovery Lab

Out-of-library synthesis, held-out verification, reuse, and cost accounting.

Each trial contains 200 tasks. Initial library: linear, square, cubic and absolute-value families. Hidden tasks also use fourth powers and x² + 2x + b. The learner must first exhaust its library, then search a predefined expression grammar. Successful expressions are retained for later tasks. A non-learning comparator searches the same grammar afresh.
