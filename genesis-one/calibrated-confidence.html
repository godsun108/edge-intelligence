Scientific limits: finite 176-program candidate universe, held-out OOD programs, synthetic ±1 noise and missingness. The new acceptance policy learns only from training examples and selects a threshold using separate validation examples. The threshold is frozen before test evaluation. Risk ceiling is a one-sided 95% Wilson upper bound on validation error among accepted proposals, not a formal deployment guarantee. A minimum of 100 validation acceptances is required. Evaluation uses the same deterministic fixed-order measurements for both policies; no oracle checks at decision time. Selection is based on the final best candidate after up to four measurements, whereas the baseline can accept early; compare both outcome and measurement costs. These are different decision schedules. Output contains per-task observations and decisions for independent audit.
