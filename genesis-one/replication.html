Genesis ONE · v8 Replication Lab

Multiple unseen random seeds, repeated-versus-novel accounting, and browser runtime measurement.

Each trial: 300 tasks, six 50-task phases, 80% dominant family. Seed changes both targets and phases' minority samples. The adaptive policy uses a 12-task rolling window of previously solved families, with no current-task family hint.

Controlled arithmetic grammar, not real-world AGI. Candidate attempts and elapsed wall time are different measures; wall time includes browser/JIT/noise and cannot be treated as CPU or energy. All strategies use the same tasks per trial. Correctness means matching six sampled inputs, not formal equivalence. Programs may repeat across tasks; novelty is counted per trial. This benchmark was designed with known phases, not a hidden test set.
