# Quantum Intelligence Boundary

EDGE should incorporate useful quantum computing as real capabilities become accessible, but never use "quantum" as a synonym for mystical certainty, superior prediction, or guaranteed financial advantage.

Appropriate future uses include:
- optimization experiments where quantum/hybrid solvers can be benchmarked against classical baselines;
- quantum random number generation when a trustworthy source/API is available and the distinction matters;
- post-quantum cryptography for protecting long-lived EDGE data;
- quantum-algorithm research tracked through SAVANT with primary-source provenance;
- simulation/research workloads where a quantum backend provides a measurable benefit.

Every quantum component must disclose whether it is:
1. classical simulation,
2. quantum-inspired classical optimization,
3. hybrid quantum/classical,
4. executed on actual quantum hardware.

No component may claim quantum advantage without a reproducible benchmark against an appropriate classical baseline.
